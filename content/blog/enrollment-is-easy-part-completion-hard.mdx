# Enrollment Is the Easy Part. Completion Is Where Students Get Lost.

Educational institutions and platforms pour enormous effort into enrollment — marketing, admissions, onboarding paperwork. But the moment a student is enrolled, the support relationship often quietly thins out, right when students most need consistent guidance to actually finish what they started.

**Why students disengage quietly**

Few students who fall behind announce it loudly. More often, they stop responding to one email, then another, and by the time anyone notices, the gap feels too large to close. Without proactive check-ins, disengagement is invisible until it's already become attrition.

**The support gap between enrollment and completion**

- Admissions questions answered quickly; ongoing academic support questions answered slowly, if at all
- No clear single point of contact for students navigating logistics, technical platform issues, or general confusion
- Support hours that don't match when students — especially working adults or international learners — are actually available
- Technical platform issues treated as IT tickets instead of urgent learning blockers

**What support built for completion looks like**

- **Proactive outreach** to students showing early signs of disengagement, not just reactive ticket handling
- **Consistent point of contact**, so students aren't re-explaining their situation to a new person every time
- **Coverage that matches student schedules**, including evenings and weekends for non-traditional learners
- **Fast resolution of technical and logistical blockers**, since these are often the quiet reason students stop showing up

**Completion rates are a support metric, not just an academic one**

For education businesses, retention from enrollment to completion is one of the clearest indicators of program quality — and support is one of the most overlooked levers for improving it.
